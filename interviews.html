PREPARE WITH PURPOSE

Show what you know.
Explain how you think.

Strong interview preparation combines fundamentals, problem-solving, project ownership, and communication. Build a routine you can repeat.

A PRACTICAL ROUTINE

Four areas to prepare.

01

Coding rounds

Practise arrays, strings, hashing, searching, sorting, and common patterns. Explain your approach before writing code.

  • Clarify the input
  • Walk through an example
  • Check edge cases
02

Core CS

Review concepts in small groups and practise explaining them without reading a definition word for word.

  • DBMS and SQL
  • Operating systems
  • Networks and OOP
03

Project discussion

Be ready to describe the problem, design decisions, trade-offs, your contribution, and what you would improve.

  • Problem and users
  • Tools and architecture
  • Challenges and learning
04

Communication

Practise concise answers that are specific and honest. If you do not know something, explain how you would investigate it.

  • Clear structure
  • Concrete examples
  • Thoughtful questions
MOCK INTERVIEW PROMPT

Practise a project explanation.

Use this structure to draft a 60–90 second answer. Your notes stay in this browser tab and are not sent anywhere.

1. Problem: What were you trying to solve?

2. Approach: How did you design and build it?

3. Contribution: Which parts did you personally complete?

4. Learning: What was challenging and what would you improve?

Notes stay on this page until you leave.
